Quality tools — practice exercise

Pick the tool that actually answers the question

This is a low-stakes practice exercise — nothing here is graded. If you haven't seen the worked example yet, start there first to see how each of the four tools answers a different kind of question.

Then come back here and try matching the tool to the question yourself. Wrong picks still get plotted, so you can see for yourself why they don't answer what was asked — and once you get a round right, you can keep clicking the other options to see why they were wrong.
